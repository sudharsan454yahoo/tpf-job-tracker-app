TPF Job Tracker: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

TPF Job Tracker reads job alert emails in your Gmail and lists the jobs in one place. This page explains what the app reads, what it keeps and how to remove it.

What the app accesses

  • Your Google account email address, to sign you in.
  • Read-only access to Gmail. The app cannot send, delete, label or change any email.
  • The app only fetches emails sent by the job portals you select in Settings. It does not fetch your other emails.

What the app stores

  • Job details taken from alert emails: role, company, location, link, portal and dates.
  • For each alert email it reads: the sender, subject, date and whether it could be read.
  • If an alert email cannot be read, its text is kept for up to 30 days so the problem can be fixed. It is then deleted.
  • The status and job descriptions you add yourself.
  • An encrypted Google access token, so the app can check for new alerts in the background.

How the data is used

  • Only to show your jobs to you and keep your list up to date.
  • It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models. No AI reads your email.
  • Other users cannot see your data.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where the data is kept

Data is stored with Supabase (database and server functions). The website is served by GitHub Pages.

Removing access and deleting your data

  • To stop access at any time, remove TPF Job Tracker at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To have your data deleted, email the address below from your Google account address. Your data will be deleted within 30 days.
